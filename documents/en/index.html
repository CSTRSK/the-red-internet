The Red Internet

A Philosophical Anatomy of Digital Authoritarianism

🇩🇪 Deutsch

About

Twelve interconnected essays tracing how the internet — conceived as a tool of emancipation — has become the primary instrument through which democratic freedoms are systematically replaced by a soft-authoritarian digital order. Not through revolution, but through software updates, behavioral gamification, and anticipatory obedience.

"Democracy wasn't abolished; it was replaced by an update that no longer permits errors." — Chapter 12: The Net as God
